Second Draft: how the call works

Call +1 (484) 740-8902. One continuous conversation, with two roles.

Mira → Fictional character → Mira

  1. Talk to Mira

    Describe the conversation you want help with. Mira listens as your AI coach.

  2. Say “Let’s practise”

    The reasoning agent uses your story to create a fictional name, situation, personality and goal. No extra confirmation or character questionnaire.

  3. The character starts

    Our controller validates the scene. Guava’s set_persona() changes the role and voice; set_task() sets the activity; add_info() supplies context. It is still the same call.

  4. Say “Hey coach”

    The scene pauses, pending changes are cancelled, and Mira’s persona and voice return. She can discuss the exchange you just had, using the conversation context and saved moment.

  5. Adjust, retry or finish

    Say “They’d joke instead” to adjust the character, “Try that moment again” to replay, or “End call.” You can also just keep talking with Mira.

Who does what?

Guava handles the phone, listening and speaking. The reasoning agent plans scene changes. Our controller applies them and prioritizes pause/end commands. Ordinary replies do not need a new planning request.

Keypad shortcuts

0 Pause · 1 Replay a saved moment from coaching · 9 End · * Help

The planner uses recent turns plus the saved scene and exchange, not unlimited history. Characters are fictional. This is not licensed therapy. Guava may retain call records.
